Gesture Synth Guide

Chords are played with two hands, each with an independent role.

Right hand — chord

  • A recognized A–G gesture (trained in Settings) selects the chord root.
  • Tilt left → major. Tilt right → minor.

Left hand — expression

  • Height → volume. Raise your hand to play louder.
  • Tilt → filter brightness. Tilt left = darker, tilt right = brighter.
  • Thumb tucked across the palm → octave up. Thumb extended out → octave down.

Setup

  • Open Settings and save at least one gesture per letter A–G for your right hand before chords will play.
  • Click "Enable Sound" once to unlock audio (browser requirement).
